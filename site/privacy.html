Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

This policy covers the website qajev.com, run by Hanamori Labs, LLC, 131 Continental Drive, Suite 305, Newark, Delaware 19713, United States ("we"). It does not cover the QAJev software you install and run on your own machine, or the model provider you choose for it.

What this website collects

  • Hosting records. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, keeps your IP address, the page requested, the time and your browser's user agent for a short period, to serve and protect the site. Cloudflare also counts visits without cookies.
  • Fonts. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when it serves them.
  • Measurement and advertising, described next.

Measurement and advertising on this website

This website uses Google Tag Manager to load Google Analytics and the advertising pixels of X, TikTok and Meta. They tell us how people find and use the site and which of our ads bring visitors here, and the ad networks may use them to show our ads to people who visited.

  • What they collect: the pages you visit and the page you came from, clicks on the buttons that copy the install command, your IP address and browser and device details, and identifiers in cookies set by Google, X, TikTok and Meta.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, nothing loads until you choose: a banner asks, with Accept and Reject side by side, and Reject loads nothing. Your answer is kept for 12 months.
  • Everywhere else, they load after the page has finished. You can turn them all off with Your privacy choices at the bottom of every page. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, we treat it as an opt-out from advertising automatically: the X, TikTok and Meta pixels stay off, and Google Analytics still counts visits unless you turn it off too.
  • Change your answer at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.
  • Google (Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics), X Corp., TikTok and Meta Platforms receive this data and use it under their own privacy policies, and may process it in the United States.
  • Legal basis: your consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland; elsewhere, our legitimate interest in measuring our website and ads, with the opt-out above.
